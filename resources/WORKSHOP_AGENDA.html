Lakelink Fiber Margin Truth: Workshop Agenda

4 hours, including kickoff and breaks. Start times assume a 9:00 AM start.

Topic Speaker Start Time Duration Description
Kickoff and Introductions Stephen 9:00 AM 15 min Welcome, introductions, and an overview of the session objectives and expected outcomes. Capture the margin questions the room cannot answer quickly today.

Break All 10:20 AM 15 min Morning break.

Break All 11:50 AM 10 min Short break.
Dashboards, Reporting, and Applications Stephen 12:00 PM 30 min Showcase the CFO margin scorecard and $1B Wall dashboard, then the Margin Truth app, where an agent-drafted savings ticket is approved and its trace and audit record are shown.
Genie One as an AI Coworker Stephen 12:30 PM 20 min Explore how Genie One supports everyday workflows, finds governed assets, answers questions, and improves productivity, using questions raised by the room at kickoff.
Wrap-up and Next Steps Stephen 12:50 PM 10 min Recap, $1B Wall challenge cards, next steps, and feedback.
